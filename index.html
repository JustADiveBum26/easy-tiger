How it started

The Story

Easy Tiger is a speakeasy room in a basement in Columbia, Missouri. The name is a nod to the Mizzou Tigers, with a little Prohibition wink on top.

It's a place to unplug after a long day. No phones, no screens. The bottles on the shelf are meant to be opened and shared, not saved for some better night.

In the room

  • Deep teal green walls and a flat black ceiling, pipes and joists included
  • One wall of burned cedar, charcoal black with silver-grey grain
  • Red leather sofa, two brown Chesterfield chairs, and a hammered copper and bronze coffee table
  • A rug with two tigers on it
  • Gold fixtures and warm amber Edison light. Nothing cool or white.
  • Exposed concrete floor, cleaned and sealed
  • A burgundy velvet curtain where a door would be
  • Prohibition-era art prints and gold animal busts on one wall
  • A record player on a dark walnut console
